  AI is pretty much the new booming market. Its prevalence in the real world and Software engineering in particular has shown itself througout the years. Just a few years ag, I had very little knowledge on AI and now it has become so mainstream that almost everyone I know uses it or at the very least knows about it. In my software engineering class (ICS 314), I pretty much only used one AI which is ChatGPT. I don't know much about any other AI though I have heard about them. They are very good at accomplishing various tasks and that includes tasks related to software engineering.

  In class, we had these experiences called WODs which were basically timed coding exercises. I was able to do a lot of them without use or very minimal use of AI. The minimal uses were typically like any syntax that I may have got off or just small adjustments. For the in class practice WODs which were the same thing except not graded, I didn't use AI to help with it until after when I can't solve it anymore. I don't remember too much of the practice WODs though. For essays I actually surprisingly didn't really use AI. Though I know essays are one of the big things people use AI for.
  
  During the first month and a half I would say I didn't rely on it too much. However, as the class progressed, the workload became increasingly taxing. I mean we had to learn Javascript, Typescript, HTML, CSS, React, and a whole plethora of other software development tools. It became increasingly difficult to manage. Plus, its not like this is the students' only class or responsibilities. For this reason, it probably caused me and a lot of students to heavily rely on AI. I had AI explain how a lot of things worked to me and it helped teached me on their functionality. Especially because in class I didn't really learn a lot of it. Rather than turning to the discord to maybe get an asnwer that may or may not satisfy my question. That's if its even answered in the first place. I found it much quicker to just ask ChatGPT how something worked or give an example if I didn't know how it worked.

  In regards to the final project, I had AI help our group basically structure how the project directories and files should look. It told me which file should go in what directory like certain .ts files should be in the API directory. It really helped on structuring the final project. Also, I had to have AI basically explain what order I should edit files because it is important in building the project. It really helped with the overall structure of the project. For errors, a lot of it was related to missing dependencies. I was able to figure out the commands to fix some of these on my own.
